description: "A practical method for turning a generic AI blog draft into a post with a point of view, from the headline to the last line."

---

An AI blog draft is usually tidy and forgettable. It has headings, balanced paragraphs and no reason to exist. Here's how to fix that without starting from scratch.

## Start by deciding the angle

Before touching a sentence, finish this line: "Most people think X, but actually Y." If you can't, the draft has no angle yet, and no amount of editing will give it one. Ask yourself what you have seen that the top search results missed.

## Replace the introduction

AI introductions restate the topic. Delete yours and start with one of these:

- A specific problem your reader has right now
- A result, with its number
- A short story from your own work

> **Before:** In today's digital age, content marketing plays a vital role in business growth.
>
> **After:** Last spring we stopped publishing weekly posts. Our traffic went up.

(That second line is an illustration of the form. If you use a claim like it, it must be true. Never make up results.)

## Cut the padding sections

Look for headings like "Why X matters" or "The importance of X". Readers who searched for how to do something already care. Delete those sections or shrink them to a sentence.

## Add proof to each section

For each heading, add at least one of these:

- A screenshot or data point you collected
- A quote from someone you spoke to
- A mistake you made and what you changed
- A clear recommendation: "Use A unless B"

This also helps with search. Google's guidance on helpful content asks whether a page shows first-hand experience, and generic text has a hard time showing it.

## Run the language pass last

When the content is right, run the post through the [blog post editor](/ai-blog-post-editor). It keeps Markdown headings, lists and links in place. Look through the edit log and pay attention to your target keywords. If a swap changes a term that readers search for, revert it.

## Check the ending

AI conclusions summarize what the reader just read. Replace yours with what to do next: one action, one link, or one honest caveat.

## Before you publish

- Would a reader learn anything here they couldn't get from the top three results?
- Are all facts checked and linked?
- Is your name on it, and would you defend every line?
- Did you follow your site's disclosure policy on AI use?
